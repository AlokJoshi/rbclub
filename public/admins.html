Administrator Options

Welcome Admins!

When a new member joins our club you can add him as a member by entering his first and last name. The system will then return to you his temporary login name and login password. Please inform the member to change his password as soon as possible.

Sometimes a member may forget his temporary login password. You can get the temporary login credentials and provide them to the member.

As an admin you can send emails individually or a group of members. To make it easy to send emails to a group of members without having to everytime select each individual member to include in the group you can create mailing lists. For instance you may create a mailing list called 'All members'. In this mailing list you would include all the members. Similarly you may include one for 'New members' in which you may include only those members who are new to our club. etc.

Once you have created these mailing lists then you just have to select the mailing list.

You can also monitor who all have received the emails that you or other admins have sent.
